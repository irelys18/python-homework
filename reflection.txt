1. Static vs. Interactive Visualizations

Static visualizations, such as regular charts and graphs, 
display information in a fixed format. They are useful when 
the goal is to present a clear result or report that does not 
require the viewer to explore the data. Interactive visualizations 
allow users to interact with the data by hovering over points, 
filtering information, zooming, and exploring different parts of a chart. 
In this assignment, the Plotly wind visualization was interactive, which 
made it easier to examine the relationship between wind strength and 
frequency for different directions.


2. Advantages of Dashboards for Real-Time Data Exploration

Dashboards are useful for real-time data exploration because they 
allow users to view multiple charts and important information in one place. 
Users can quickly identify trends, compare values, and monitor changes as 
new data becomes available. Dashboards can also include filters and interactive 
features that allow users to focus on the information that is most important to 
them. This can help organizations make faster and more informed decisions.


3. Plotly and Professional Data Communication

Interactive tools such as Plotly can improve data communication in professional 
settings because they allow people to explore data instead of only looking at a 
static chart. Users can hover over data points to see specific values, identify 
patterns, and better understand relationships between variables. Interactive 
visualizations can make complex data easier to understand for both technical 
and non-technical audiences. Plotly can therefore help analysts communicate 
their findings more clearly and effectively.
