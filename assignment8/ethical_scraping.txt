Wikipedia restricts crawlers from areas such as /w/, /api/, /trap/, and some Special pages. It also blocks access to some pages about 
deletions, copyright, spam, and administrative discussions.

Yes, some user agents, such as MJ12bot, wget, and WebReaper, are completely blocked. SemrushBot has a 5-second delay between requests.

Websites use robots.txt to communicate rules to automated web crawlers about which areas they should or should not access.
It helps websites manage crawler traffic, protect resources, and prevent bots from accessing sensitive or unnecessary areas.
Following robots.txt is an important part of responsible and ethical web scraping.
