Task 6 Challenges


One challenge I had was finding the correct elements 
on the web pages using Selenium. At first, my XPath 
for the OWASP page selected too many links, including 
navigation links. I solved this by using a more specific 
XPath that targets only the A01 through A10 vulnerabilities.
Another challenge was making sure the book scraper collected 
the title, author, and format-year information correctly. 
I solved this by finding the specific elements inside each 
book result and storing the information in dictionaries before 
creating the DataFrame.
